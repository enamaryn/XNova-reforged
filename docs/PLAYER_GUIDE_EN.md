# Player guide — XNova Reforged

> Version `v0.2.0` (private alpha). This guide describes what the game actually does today. Languages: [Français](GUIDE_JOUEUR.md) · [English](PLAYER_GUIDE_EN.md) · [Español](PLAYER_GUIDE_ES.md) · [Deutsch](PLAYER_GUIDE_DE.md) · [Italiano](PLAYER_GUIDE_IT.md).

## 1. Getting started

1. **Create your account.** Your email address must be confirmed: click the link you receive by email (valid for 24 h, it can be resent from the sign-in page).
2. You receive a **starting planet** ("Home Planet") with 500 metal, 500 crystal and no buildings.
3. Follow the **getting-started guide** on the overview page: 11 goals computed from your real situation, each with a link to the action to take. You can hide it once finished.
4. Pick your **language** (French, English, Spanish, German, Italian) with the selector in the header or in *Options*. It follows the page address (`/fr/…`, `/de/…`).

## 2. Server pace

Costs and formulas are those of the original game; the **server speed** speeds up durations and production (an administrator setting). The reference profile is **×50**:

| Milestone for a reasonable player | Time at ×50 |
|---|---:|
| Research Laboratory finished | ≈ 22 min |
| First research started | ≈ 1 h |
| Shipyard finished | ≈ 1 h 05 |
| First ship | ≈ 1 h 50 |

These times were measured on the server with a player who only spends what they produce. On a ×1 server everything is about 50 times slower: plan for long waits.

## 3. Resources and energy

- **Metal**: buildings, ships, defenses. **Crystal**: laboratory, research, ships. **Deuterium**: fleet fuel, laboratory and most research (you start with none: the deuterium synthesizer is your priority).
- **Energy**: mines consume the energy of solar plants. If consumption exceeds production, **all** mines slow down proportionally. Keep production at 100%.
- Each planet has a small base income, independent of mines.
- **Storage** is limited (base capacity of one million per resource, growing with storage buildings); beyond it, production is lost.

## 4. Build, research, produce

- **Buildings**: the cost is paid at launch and grows with each level. The number of simultaneous constructions per planet is limited: 1 at the start, 2 with the *Construction Management* research, 3 with a level-50 commander as well.
- **Research**: one at a time per player, paid at launch. It needs a laboratory level and sometimes other technologies (missing requirements are shown). Start with *Energy*.
- **Shipyard and defense**: orders are paid immediately and built in batches. A **waiting** order can be withdrawn: 90% of the resources paid are refunded. A batch already started finishes normally. *Parallel Production* adds production lines.
- Requirements (shipyard level, technologies) are shown on each card.

## 5. Fleets and missions

*Fleet* page: choose ships, coordinates `[galaxy:system:position]`, a mission and the speed. Fuel (deuterium) is debited on departure.

| Mission | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Transport** | delivers the cargo to the target planet, then the fleet returns |
| **Deploy** | settles ships and cargo on **your** destination planet, no return |
| **Attack** | combat; loot possible |
| **Espionage** | report on the target (more probes or a better *Espionage* technology = more details) |
| **Colonization** | founds a colony on a free position with a colony ship (21 planets maximum) |

A fleet in flight can be **recalled** from *Movements*. Results arrive in *Reports*.

## 6. Combat

- 6 rounds at most; some ships have **rapid fire** against other types.
- 30% of the cost of destroyed ships becomes **debris**.
- Each destroyed **defense** is repaired with a 70% chance after the battle.
- **Loot**: up to 50% of the target's resources, **limited to the space left in the winner's surviving ships**; the rest is not carried off.
- Tips: spy before attacking, check cargo capacity, do not let your resources pile up.

## 7. Galaxy

- 9 galaxies × 499 systems × 15 positions. The *Galaxy* page opens on the system of your active planet; your planet is highlighted.
- Occupied position: spy, attack, transport. Free position: colonize.

## 8. Commander, power, ranking

- Your **commander level** (1 to 100) depends only on your buildings and research; your **power** also includes ships, defenses and colonies. Stockpiles do not count.
- The **ranking** is based on power; your rank appears in the header and on the overview.

## 9. Social

- **Messages** between players (by username).
- **Alliances**: create one (tag of 2 to 8 characters) or join one by invitation; the founder cannot leave without dissolving it.

## 10. Account and security

- Password: at least 8 characters with a lowercase letter, an uppercase letter and a digit. You can change it, and your email address (confirmation by link), in *Options*.
- "Forgot password" sends a link valid for one hour. Changing or resetting the password signs out your other devices.
- Too many failed sign-in attempts temporarily block access.

## 11. Known alpha limits

No officers, market or events; game names and descriptions and some error messages stay in French whatever the chosen language; emails are sent in French.
